The regulated wind down

A 33 year old deposit taking NBFC, founded by a former Executive Director of a national bank, with 27 unbroken years of dividends behind it. Then the founder passed away, the pandemic damaged the books, and the Reserve Bank of India ordered a halt to all lending and deposit taking. I was 22, and I led operations and compliance through what came next.

The situation

Every stakeholder a product manager fears, all at once, all real

Product managers talk about stakeholder pressure. This was the uncapped version of it. The central bank had frozen the core business. Nearly 3,000 depositors, from farmers to public servants, were owed money and knew it. Some threatened legal action and criminal escalation. Some threatened the office premises and the safety of the people inside. The Ministry of Corporate Affairs wanted its filings. Courts and the company law tribunal held the recovery path. Twelve remaining employees needed to be paid and kept safe. And prospective investors examining a takeover wanted clean data from a company that until recently kept its records on steno typewriters and manual ledgers.

None of these audiences could be deprioritised, because each one held a veto over the outcome. That constraint, in which every stakeholder can stop the ship, is the defining condition of the whole project.

The response

Deadlines set by a regulator do not move

The sequence below ran under a clock nobody inside the company controlled. Regulatory returns have statutory dates. Court hearings are scheduled by the court. Depositors assembling outside a branch do not wait for a sprint boundary. The response was built the way an incident response is built. Stabilise the people first, then the data, then the process, and communicate on a cadence throughout.

What I owned

Five workstreams, one person accountable for each landing

Depositor trust

Met depositors in person at branches across Tamil Nadu, explained the true state of affairs, evidenced that no funds were mismanaged, and stood up a prompt grievance redressal mechanism that kept individual anger from becoming collective escalation.

Regulator relationships

Main liaison to the RBI's Department of Supervision and the Registrar of Companies at MCA, alongside CIBIL and IDBI Trusteeship. Every mandated return filed before deadline, while sitting across the table from authority figures with 40 years in the industry.

The data foundation

Digitised a 30 year loans and deposits history maintained on steno typewriters and manual ledgers, with a team of 10, then built the reporting on top of it: interest patterns, tax liabilities and the RBI's prudential norms.

Runway and people

Resized the team from 30 to 12, cutting monthly burn 36%, with every departing employee's gratuity protected through an external LIC trustee. Reported to the board on a fixed cadence for transparency.

Legal and investor tracks

Recovery strategy with the ex Public Prosecutor of Tamil Nadu, resolution professionals at the NCLT for the legal takeover, and term sheet, credit policy and ownership analysis across M&A conversations with prospective investors.

The sunset plan

Drafted the company's exit strategy for submission to the Reserve Bank of India, and the Ministry granted a relaxation and moratorium that acknowledged the team's handling. Partial recovery of the asset book funded phased repayments to public creditors.

Why this belongs in a product portfolio

The same work, in the language product teams use

Nothing here shipped an app. All of it built the muscles product managers are actually hired for, under stakes most product roles never reach. The mapping below is literal, not decorative.

PMCrisis communications and incident response. Owning the room when users have lost trust, saying the true thing, and giving people a working escalation path instead of a promise.

The takeaway

What this proves that a case study cannot

The case studies on this site show how I think. Newlink shows how I operate when the downside is real. Deadlines set by regulators, users who can show up at the office, data that had to be built before it could be read, and six stakeholder groups holding vetoes. The outcome was a moratorium granted on the strength of the team's handling, zero missed filings, and repayments flowing to the people who were owed.

That is the profile of work a product manager does in a regulated or high stakes domain, fintech, health, marketplaces with real money in motion. I have already done it once, before the title.
